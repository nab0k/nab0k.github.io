01 / After the signing

Signing is the easy part.

Keeping a partnership alive takes four things.

01

One shared picture

Goals, roles, timelines and what success means — written down, and seen the same way by both sides.

02

Rhythm and early warning

Regular check-ins and transparency, so problems surface while they are still small. No surprises.

03

Two speeds, one plan

Ukrainian teams change their products week by week; European partners work through approvals. I explain each side’s logic to the other.

04

Renegotiate, not break up

When plans change: a new compromise, a missing partner or supplier, a new version of the deal.

02 / What you get

Two sides, one person between them.

I work in person across the EU, and with Ukraine remotely — through my network and partner teams. UK and US meetings by arrangement.

For international companies working with Ukraine

Your person on the Ukrainian side

  • Map the Ukrainian market. Who makes what, which companies are real, where demand comes from — military units, Brave1 Market, DOT-Chain, state procurement.
  • Find and check Ukrainian partners. Manufacturers and suppliers that fit your product, capacity and compliance needs — identified, checked and introduced.
  • Tell your Ukraine story. Strategic communications for your Ukrainian partnership, programme or market entry.
For Ukrainian companies going to the EU

Your person in Europe

  • International communications. Positioning, materials and messages that European customers, partners and media understand.
  • Partners and buyers in the EU. Finding, checking and first conversations with distributors, integrators and manufacturers; export readiness.
  • Presence in Europe. Partner meetings and presence at European exhibitions — where your team cannot be all the time.
